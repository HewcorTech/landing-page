Structured Cabling and Network Infrastructure

Structured Cabling and Network Infrastructure

Custom-Designed Infrastructure:

  • Get a tailored network layout engineered specifically for your building's footprint, ensuring strong connectivity in every workspace.

Clean Network Solutions:

  • We build highly organized server racks, patch panels, and neat cable pathways to keep your network infrastructure manageable and easy to troubleshoot.

Premium Cabling Brands:

  • We only install quality copper and fibre. All structural installations utilize industry-standard hardware from CommScope, Corning, Belden, APC, Primex, and EATON.

Network Setup and Programming:

  • Functional networks require programming: configuring secure firewalls, setting up Virtual LANs (VLANs) to protect sensitive company data, establishing backup internet connections, and programming your switches to power and manage your devices efficiently.

High-Speed Foundations:

  • We utilize high-performance CAT6 cabling, fibre-optic backbones, and cutting-edge Wi-Fi access points to deliver maximum bandwidth and zero bottlenecking across your workspace.

Top-Tier Networking Hardware:

  • Get full integration and ongoing support for enterprise-grade hardware from brands you can trust, including SonicWALL, Cisco, TP-Link, Meraki, and Ubiquiti.

Managed IT & Network Security

Managed IT & Network Security

Network & Cyber Security:

  • We protect your business data and intellectual property from digital threats like ransomware, malware, and phishing. We deploy and manage enterprise-grade firewalls, secure VPNs, and active threat detection utilizing industry-standard tools like Meraki and Cisco Umbrella. On-Site & Remote Technical Support: Get help when you need it. Our certified technicians provide remote troubleshooting and on-site support to resolve day-to-day IT issues and minimize downtime.

System Monitoring & Maintenance:

  • We monitor your workstations, servers, and NAS configurations. We keep critical software updated, verify backup runs, and maintain your existing hardware so it continues running at peak performance.

Software & Cloud Administration:

  • We manage the setup and access control for your business software. This includes provisioning licenses and configuring secure user access for major platforms like Microsoft 365, Adobe, Oracle, and SAP, as well as mapping shared office printers and scanners.

IT Documentation:

  • We handle employee tech transitions to make hiring and departures seamless. When a new hire starts, we get them configured on day one; when an employee leaves, we instantly revoke their access to keep your company data secure. We keep all of this recorded in clear, up-to-date network documentation.

Servers & Workstations

Servers & Workstations

Performance Estimation & Planning:

  • We consult with your team to evaluate your operational requirements, estimating your processor, memory, and storage needs so you only buy what you actually need.

End-to-End Procurement:

  • We handle the purchasing of business-grade laptops, desktops, custom enterprise servers, and network-attached storage (NAS) devices. Pre-Configuration & Testing: Every computer and server is fully assembled, updated, software-imaged, and tested by our team beforehand to guarantee a fast, plug-and-play setup on delivery day.

Enterprise Workstation & Server Deployment:

  • We handle the physical installation, clean desk cable management for workstations, and secure rack-mounting and configuration of NAS units and enterprise servers.

Existing Hardware Optimization:

  • We troubleshoot your existing computers and servers, installing smart hardware upgrades such as SSD swaps and memory expansions to stretch your current budget and extend device lifespan.

Leading Enterprise Brands:

  • We standardize your office technology using highly reliable, business-class hardware from trusted manufacturers like Dell, Lenovo, and Synology.

Commercial Electrical Services

Commercial Electrical Services

Certified Journeyman Electricians:

  • All on-site wiring, troubleshooting, and electrical installations are executed by fully certified commercial electricians, ensuring the work is safe, clean, and done right.

Scalable Project Support:

  • We scale our services to match your exact scope of work, whether you need to add a few dedicated outlets for a new desk layout or completely rewire a space during a major commercial renovation.

Power & Lighting Layouts:

  • We install tailored power distribution systems, dedicated appliance circuits, and energy-efficient commercial LED lighting setups designed to keep your workspace bright, productive, and fully operational.

Low-Voltage System Capabilities
