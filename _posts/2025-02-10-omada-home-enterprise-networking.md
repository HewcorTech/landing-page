## Home + Light Enterprise = The sweet spot

You might think “enterprise networking” = big building, racks, endless cables. Not here. “Light enterprise” fits because:

- Many homes are basically mini-offices now—remote work, online school, smart devices everywhere.  
- Using robust gear means fewer compromises: no strange dead zones, no overloaded guest networks, no constant resetting.  
- If your business grows—maybe you’re the neighbourhood IT pro or adding an office space—your infrastructure scales with you.

A typical setup includes:

- One Omada controller (hardware or virtual) to tie everything together  
- Multiple Omada access points placed for ideal coverage  
- Managed switches or gateways as needed  
- Segmented networks: guest, IoT, and main—for performance and security
